Open, end to end

Telegram

Where you talk to your coach. A daily cron check-in plus anything you ask.

Hermes Agent

Open-source agent runtime in Docker. The training-coach skill runs the five-step loop and the approval rule.

wearable-mcp

Ours, in Python. Garmin reads and writes, guardrails.py against goals.yaml, and two-phase writes: propose_plan stores a hashed plan, apply_plan executes exactly that plan. Brand-agnostic provider interface.

Composio

Google Calendar: free slots, events, and the [Coach] entries.

Parallel

Web search when the plan needs the outside world, like a hotel gym on a trip.
