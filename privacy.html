Privacy Policy — enzo_bot

Last updated: October 22, 2026

enzo_bot is a personal, single-user assistant built and operated by Enzo Patelli Junqueira for his own use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app reads and, when the owner asks, writes data in the owner's own Google account: Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs and Sheets.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to assist the owner (for example, surfacing deadlines found in his own email and placing events on his own calendar). It is processed on hardware the owner controls and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. Message and event content is processed transiently and is not retained beyond what the owner's assistant needs for the task at hand.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
